# Ready-to-use prompts

These prompts assume Grabblist is already connected through MCP.

## Save carefully

> Inspect this URL and save it to Grabblist as a static snapshot. Use only facts present on the page, omit unsupported optional fields, and tell me what you could not verify. Do not invent prices, discounts, ratings, or condition.

## Find previous research

> Search my Grabblist for items related to [topic]. Return the most relevant matches with saved price, status, tags, and collection membership. Clearly label prices as stored snapshots rather than current prices.

## Compare a shortlist

> Find my saved [product type] shortlist. Read the full item records and linked alternatives, then compare them by [criteria]. Separate stored facts from your current external research, identify missing evidence, and recommend a next step without changing statuses.

## Build under a budget

> Create a collection for [goal] with a budget of [amount and currency]. Research candidates, save only evidence-backed snapshots, add them to the collection, flag compatibility gaps, and keep the total under budget. Ask before pinning top picks.

## Recheck prices

> Recheck the current public prices of the items in [collection]. Match exact variants, seller, condition, quantity, currency, and shipping treatment. Update Grabblist only when the comparison is valid, use `source="ai_check"`, and summarize every changed and unchanged item.

## Record a decision

> Show me the alternatives linked to [item]. Summarize the decision evidence and ask which status I want: considering, shortlisted, decided, bought, or rejected. Apply the status only after I answer and record a short reason.

## Safe cleanup

> Find possible duplicate items in my Grabblist. Show the candidates and explain whether they are true duplicates, alternatives, or the same seller. Do not delete or link anything until I confirm the exact actions.

## Share deliberately

> Review [collection] for notes or context that should not be public. Summarize what a public link would expose and ask for confirmation before enabling sharing.
